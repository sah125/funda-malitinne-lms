How it works

From enquiry to certificate

1. Enquire or register

Ask about a programme or register on Funda Malitinne. Employers and funders can partner with us for a whole group.

2. We approve you

Our team checks your registration and entry requirements, then emails you a secure login link.

3. Learn anywhere

Work through knowledge modules online, attend contact sessions where required and complete your workplace logbook.

4. Build your PoE

Submit activities and evidence digitally. Registered assessors and moderators sign off each module.

5. Get certified

Complete the external integrated summative assessment for your QCTO certificate.
