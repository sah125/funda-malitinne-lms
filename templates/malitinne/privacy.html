How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Malitinne (Pty) Ltd (“Malitinne”, “we”) is the responsible party for personal information processed through this website and the Funda Malitinne learning platform. We comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

This notice is a starting template written for Malitinne's systems. Please have it reviewed by your Information Officer or legal advisor and complete the registration details with the Information Regulator before relying on it.

What we collect

  • Enquiries: name, email, phone, organisation and your message.
  • Learner registration: name, username, email, phone, ID number, date of birth, gender, nationality, preferred language and, if you choose to tell us, disability information (special personal information).
  • Learning records: enrolments, progress, assessment submissions, portfolio-of-evidence uploads, attendance, logbooks and certificates.
  • Applications for opportunities: CV, qualifications, ID document and the details on the application form.
  • Security logs: sign-in attempts, IP address and browser type, used to protect accounts.

Why we use it

  • To review and approve registrations and give you access to your courses.
  • To deliver, assess and certify training as required by the QCTO, SETAs and funders.
  • To report on funded programmes where the funder or law requires it (for example SETA learner reporting).
  • To respond to enquiries and partnership requests.
  • To keep the platform secure and prevent fraud.

We only process personal information with your consent, to perform our agreement with you or your funder, or where the law requires it. We do not sell your information.

Who can see it

Only authorised Malitinne staff, your facilitators and assessors, and — where your programme requires it — the relevant quality council, SETA or funder. Learners cannot see other learners' personal details. Every access to learner documents and exports is logged.

How we protect it

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS) and accounts must be approved by our team before they can sign in.
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes; repeated failed sign-ins are temporarily blocked.
  • Sessions expire automatically and private pages are never cached by your browser.
  • Uploads are restricted to safe document types and size limits.

How long we keep it

Learner achievement records are kept for as long as quality councils and SETAs require for verification. Enquiries that do not lead to a programme are deleted after 24 months. You may ask us to delete information we are not legally required to keep.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, object to processing, or withdraw consent. Email {{ CONTACT_EMAIL }} with the subject “POPIA request”. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.
