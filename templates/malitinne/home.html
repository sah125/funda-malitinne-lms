Our Founder
Driven by Purpose, Guided by Passion

Sibusisiwe Kraai founded Malitinne after recognizing that many youths — especially from disadvantaged backgrounds — were not privy to the abundant opportunities available through government skills development interventions.

Sibu identified the mismatch between the skills job seekers have and those required by industry, and built Malitinne to bridge that gap. Within 5 years, Malitinne placed over 10,000 graduates and apprentices in over 2,850 workplaces across South Africa.

Malitinne is always seeking partnerships through corporates, NGOs, and public or private institutions in developing the skills of especially the youth — giving them meaning in life and creating better, more livable communities.
