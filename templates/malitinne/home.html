About Malitinne

Malitinne is a South African black-women owned Human Capital Development company. Our roots are in placement of learners in apprenticeship programmes. However, we have grown to offer holistic turnkey skills development and training solutions.

We are accredited with several SETAs for learnerships and short-skills programmes. Malitinne also offers non-accredited training programmes such as workplace readiness, etiquette and emotional intelligence.
