About Malitinne

Grooming human capital for the workplace

Malitinne helps people and organisations turn learning into practical capability, sustainable employment and stronger communities.

Who we are

Malitinne (Pty) Ltd is a Durban-based, black-women owned skills development company serving learners, employers, municipalities and public-sector partners.

Our purpose

We bridge available and required skills through structured programmes, learner support, placements and project delivery.

How we work

Our programmes combine accredited learning with coordination, evidence, mentoring and workplace experience so that participants can apply what they learn.

Our standards

We work across SETA-aligned programmes and maintain a practical focus on quality, accountability and meaningful learner outcomes.
