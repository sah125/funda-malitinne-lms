How the QCTO model maps online
  • KM Knowledge modules → online lessons, read on any device
  • IAC Formative activities → online assignments (upload answers)
  • PM Practical modules → live or recorded sessions + observation checklists signed by the assessor
  • WM Work experience → digital logbook signed by the workplace supervisor
  • PoE Everything collects into the learner's digital Portfolio of Evidence
